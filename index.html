DEEP RESEARCH ENGINE

WIE — Web Intelligence Engine (Deep Research Pipeline)

An autonomous research system that takes complex open-ended hypotheses, deconstructs them into atomic search queries, executes parallel web discovery, scrapes and extracts semantic content, reranks findings, and synthesizes citation-grounded reports.

WHAT IT IS

An automated multi-agent deep research pipeline simulating the workflow of human investigative research analysts.

WHY IT EXISTS

Single-prompt LLM web searches suffer from shallow crawling, noisy ad-infested DOMs, and unsourced statements.

HOW IT WORKS

Planner decomposes hypotheses → crawler fans out → cross-encoder reranks top paragraphs → evaluator synthesizes report.

WHAT MAKES IT SPECIAL

Recursive citation tracking with automatic corroboration scoring and headless scraper resilience against anti-bot challenges.
